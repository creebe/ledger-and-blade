# Somebody dies

A good squad with a full pack goes all the way down to the College Sink and the Kiln Engine. They are not quite good enough yet.

Seed 3001. Long expedition: To the bottom of the College Sink, where the Kiln Engine walks.

## The preparation

1. **Ash Stroud**, Vanguard: Arming Sword, Mail Hauberk
2. **Leofric Orrin**, Skirmisher: Spear, Gambeson
3. **Idris Abernoy**, Chaplain: Flanged Mace, Gambeson
4. **Ewan Brack**, Arcanist: Plain Focus Rod, Padded Robes

**Pack:** 6 food, 11 lamp oil, 6 bandages, 4 tools, 6 reagents, 2 wet masks.

**Orders:** turn back: steady; heal first: worst; wandering monsters: avoid; camp: at camp sites; supplies: sparingly.

## The log
