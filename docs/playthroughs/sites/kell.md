# Kell Mines: sample expeditions

Silver workings in the northern hills, half of them flooded, and the deepest of them held by people who never paid for it.

Zones: the upper workings, the Sump, the Trevick level. Boss: throw Gaffer Moyle off the Trevick level.

Written by `npm run exp:logs`: a seasoned squad of four (level 3, rested) on a medium trip and on the long trip to the boss. Log lines as the player reads them; bold lines are the ones the game bolds.

## Ore by the Sack: Bring back 3 sacks of silver ore

_Medium route. Outcome: complete, 9 of 9 rooms, job done._


**Week 1**

- Ulla walks at the front for the first mile, until the novelty wears off. It is a long way to Kell Mines.

**Week 2**

- It is another week on the road. Ulla has worked out which boot is letting the water in, and it is both of them.

**Week 3**

- They reach Kell Mines with two weeks of work ahead of them and food for about that. Bertil leads off.
- **They go into the upper workings. Water drips from the roof, and the timbers creak when they pass under them.**
- The lamps go up. The light reaches about four yards down the gallery and gives up.
- The water on the gallery floor moves, and the cave crab climbs out of it claws first.
- Edda catches the cave crab with the spear and something cracks. _(2, it shrugs)_
- Edda comes back the other way and catches the cave crab. _(2, it shrugs)_
- Bertil gets out of the way of the cave crab.
- Bertil batters the cave crab with the flat of the arming sword. _(3, it hates that)_
- The cave crab goes down and is slow getting up.
- Ulla flicks a blade into the cave crab from the back. _(2, it shrugs)_
- Brenna shoots between two shoulders and hits the cave crab. _(4, it shrugs)_
- Edda's blow lands on nothing.
- Edda cuts again, faster, and finds the cave crab. _(2, it shrugs)_
- Bertil chops at the cave crab and connects. _(4, it shrugs)_
- Brenna's arrow goes over the cave crab and into the distance.
- Ulla throws a knife over the front rank. It sticks in the cave crab. _(1, it shrugs)_
- Edda steps in and hits the cave crab hard with the spear. _(2, it shrugs)_
- Edda follows through and opens the cave crab up a second time. _(1, it shrugs)_
- Brenna's shot goes over the front rank and into the cave crab. _(3, it shrugs)_
- Brenna finishes the cave crab with a blow behind the head.
- They wipe the blades on whatever is nearest and count the bandages.
- "Nobody's working here," says the first claim-jumper, standing in front of a fresh heap of ore.
- Ulla asks the first claim-jumper how much they are being paid. It is not enough, and once they have all agreed on that, they leave.
- The gallery shrinks until they are walking bent double. Ulla counts the props as they go, to have something to count.
- While the others rest, Brenna goes through a heap of junk by the path and comes back with a load of iron. _(a load of iron)_
- They follow an old tramway into the hill. The rails have rusted to the sleepers, and Edda trips on most of them.
- They eat standing up. Ulla passes an onion round, which is generous of them, and nobody thanks them for it. _(1 food)_
- **They make camp in the old powder store, because it is dry and has a door. Nobody lights a pipe.**
- They get a small fire going under a pot. Wounds get looked at, and people sleep where they sit. _(1 food)_

**Week 4**

- **They climb down a ladder into the Sump. The water is at their knees at the bottom, and it gets colder as they go.**
- They light the lamps at the mouth of the adit. Kell is not improved by being able to see it.
- Somebody signals, and they wait while the culvert rats go past.
- They come to a junction of four galleries, all flooded, with a ladder going up into the dark.
- Brenna feels along the bottom of a flooded cut and comes up with 2 loads of iron. _(2 loads of iron)_
- They wade along a drowned gallery with the roof a hand's breadth above the lamps. Edda hums to keep their teeth from chattering.
- They pass an old pump, still fixed to the wall, with a nest of something in the workings.
- They stop and eat cold bread and hard cheese. Bertil says it is the best meal of their life. _(1 food)_
- Somebody's buckle clinks, and the pit ponies turn round.
- There is a pony down here, shaggy and grey with dust. The pit pony looks at the squad's packs, and then at the squad, and charges.
- **They walk straight into it. The pit pony has the first move.**
- The pit pony bites Bertil on the arm and shakes. _(16)_
- Edda finds an opening and hits the pit pony in it. _(8, it hates that)_
- Edda tries a second strike and nearly loses the spear.
- Brenna looses, and the arrow takes the pit pony. _(11, it hates that)_
- Bertil gets the arming sword into the pit pony and twists. _(10)_
- Bertil gets the pit pony in the right place, and it drops.
- The last of them goes down. Somebody laughs, too loudly.
- They reach the drowned pump house the job named. The great beam engine is still there, rusted solid, and somebody is living in its boiler.
- The first pit pony comes out of the dark with its ears back and its teeth showing. It has not seen daylight in years, and it hasn't missed it.
- Edda goes in low, and the second pit pony is not there.
- Bertil brings the arming sword down on the second pit pony. _(8)_
- The first pit pony snaps at Bertil and gets nothing.
- The second pit pony goes for Bertil and overshoots.
- A bolt from Brenna thumps into the second pit pony. _(14, it hates that)_
- Ulla throws a knife over the front rank. It sticks in the second pit pony. _(8, it hates that)_
- Ulla puts the second pit pony down at last.
- Bertil puts their weight behind the arming sword, and the first pit pony takes all of it. _(8)_
- Bertil jabs at the first pit pony, and it backs off.
- Edda swings the spear into the first pit pony and keeps swinging. _(8, it hates that)_
- Edda's second strike finds the first pit pony. _(10, it hates that)_
- Brenna doesn't have a clear shot and takes it anyway. It misses.
- Ulla flicks a blade into the first pit pony from the back. _(7, it hates that)_
- The first pit pony limps off a few yards and falls over.
- It is over. They stand there breathing hard and counting each other.
- Ulla wades out of the pump house with 4 sacks of silver ore held above their head. _(4 sacks of silver ore)_
- The way out of the hill feels shorter than the way in, which it is not.

**Week 5**

- **The squad walks back into Varrow footsore, with the job done. Ulla goes straight to the Copper Kettle and is not seen again until morning.**

_Home: nobody lost. Deeds: Completed a medium expedition (+2 renown); Fought off a wandering pack (+1 renown)._

## Moyle's Vein: Throw Gaffer Moyle off the Trevick level

_Long route. Outcome: lost, 13 of 14 rooms, job not done._


**Week 1**

- Kit walks at the front for the first mile, until the novelty wears off. It is a long way to Kell Mines.

**Week 2**

- Kit takes the first step into Kell Mines. The farmer who gave them a bed last night told them not to go, and said it twice.
- **Kell's upper galleries were cut a century ago and left when the silver went deeper. Nobody told the rats.**
- The lamps go up. The light reaches about four yards down the gallery and gives up.
- They pass a ladder going down into a flooded shaft. The water is black and very still, and nobody wants to know how deep it is.
- They walk past a face covered in old pick marks. Somebody has chalked a tally on it, and the tally stops at forty.
- Bertil notices a hollow under a tree root with a load of iron stuffed into it. _(a load of iron)_
- There is a side gallery here with a door on it, which is unusual in a mine.
- Kit picks through a heap of spoil and comes out with a purse of old coin. _(a purse of old coin)_
- **They climb down a ladder into the Sump. The water is at their knees at the bottom, and it gets colder as they go.**
- They light the lamps at the mouth of the adit. Kell is not improved by being able to see it.
- They find a wayside shrine to Saint Oda. Isolde says the words over it and makes everyone sit down for a while. _(−10 fatigue each)_
- They eat standing up. Freya passes an onion round, which is generous of them, and nobody thanks them for it. _(1 food)_
- There is a pony down here, shaggy and grey with dust. The first pit pony looks at the squad's packs, and then at the squad, and charges.
- Kit batters the first pit pony with the flat of the arming sword. _(6)_
- The first pit pony reels and stands there with no idea where it is.
- A bolt from Bertil thumps into the first pit pony. _(13, it hates that)_
- Isolde tries to push forward past the others and gets an elbow for it.
- Kit gets out of the way of the second pit pony.
- Freya speaks the working and it hits the first pit pony square. _(13)_
- The first pit pony gives a last kick and lies still.
- Kit swings the arming sword into the second pit pony and keeps swinging. _(9)_
- The wind takes Bertil's arrow wide of the second pit pony.
- Isolde can't reach anything from back here and shouts advice instead.
- The second pit pony gets its teeth into Kit. _(22)_
- Freya gets the blow they were waiting for. The second pit pony feels all of it. _(20)_
- Freya gets the second pit pony in the right place, and it drops.
- They wipe the blades on whatever is nearest and count the bandages.

**Week 3**

- They come to a junction of four galleries, all flooded, with a ladder going up into the dark.
- In an ore wagon tipped on its side in the water, Isolde finds 2 sacks of silver ore. _(2 sacks of silver ore)_
- They wade along a drowned gallery with the roof a hand's breadth above the lamps. Bertil hums to keep their teeth from chattering.
- Bertil stops at a cairn by the path, moves two stones and finds a sack of silver ore under them. _(a sack of silver ore)_
- **They camp on a ledge above the water in the old stables, on dry straw that is twenty years old.**
- There is hot food, and the chaplain goes round everyone with a lamp and a needle. By morning they are in better shape than they deserve. _(1 food)_
- **The Trevick level was cut by a mine captain who died rich. The vein runs along the wall at shoulder height, and it is still there.**
- They light the lamps, and the flames lean toward the deep galleries where the air is moving. Nobody likes it.
- Lamps come round the bend in the gallery. The first claim-jumper looks up from a seam that is not theirs to dig and reaches for a pick handle.
- Bertil times it perfectly and the powder man pays for it. _(14)_
- Isolde is too far back to hit anything, and knows it.
- Kit ducks, and the first claim-jumper goes stumbling past.
- Kit steps in and hits the second claim-jumper hard with the arming sword. _(10)_
- The powder man catches Kit with a knife on the backswing. _(18)_
- Freya shouts for everyone to get their heads down, and lets the plain working go wide. _(2 doses)_
- Freya opens a hand and the focus spits at the first claim-jumper. _(7)_
- Freya's spell splashes off the stones beside the second claim-jumper.
- Freya lets go of the working. The powder man is in the way of it. _(5)_
- The second claim-jumper kicks Bertil in the knee and hits them while they are bent over. _(17)_
- Kit finds an opening and hits the second claim-jumper in it. _(11)_
- The first claim-jumper gets in close and lands a hard one on Bertil. _(16)_
- The second claim-jumper gets a blade into Kit. _(10)_
- In the scuffle the second claim-jumper gets away with a sack of silver ore.
- Bertil shoots, and the powder man moves at the last moment.
- The powder man clubs Isolde across the side of the head. _(13)_
- Isolde stitches Bertil up in three movements, with big crooked stitches that hold anyway. _(+11)_
- The air goes tight around Freya, and then the powder man takes the spell full on. _(10)_
- Freya gets the powder man on the ground, and the powder man decides that is where they will stay.
- Bertil looses, and the arrow takes the second claim-jumper. _(9)_
- The second claim-jumper sits down hard and says they have had enough. Bertil takes that as a yes.
- The first claim-jumper drives the point into Bertil's shoulder. _(10)_
- The first claim-jumper grabs a load of iron out of a pack and runs off with it.
- Kit chops at the first claim-jumper and connects. _(11)_
- Isolde drags Bertil back a yard and ties off the worst of the bleeding. _(+11)_
- Freya finds the weak place on the first claim-jumper, and it goes in deep. _(20)_
- Freya puts the first claim-jumper down, and the first claim-jumper stays down.
- They win that one. Nobody says anything for a while.
- Isolde sits Kit down on a step and does the job properly. _(+16)_
- The gallery opens into the old captain's office, cut into the rock, with a desk and a portrait gone black with damp.
- Isolde finds a purse of old coin in a sack at the bottom of the windlass, tied and waiting. _(a purse of old coin)_

**Week 4**

- Bertil sees the props ahead are rotten through and shores them up with a spare length of timber before anyone goes under. _(1 tools)_
- They share out bread and the end of a sausage. Bertil makes theirs last as long as it can. _(1 food)_
- Somebody's buckle clinks, and the powder men turn round.
- The powder man lights a fuse off a candle, looks up and sees them, and thinks about it.
- **The powder man gets the first blow in before anyone sees it coming.**
- The powder man drives the point into Kit's shoulder. _(16)_
- Bertil picks a spot on the powder man and puts an arrow in it. _(9)_
- Isolde gets a dressing on Kit, tight and fast. _(+11)_
- Kit puts their weight behind the arming sword, and the powder man takes all of it. _(9)_
- Freya flicks a spark at the powder man and misses.
- Bertil's shot goes over the front rank and into the powder man. _(10)_
- The powder man drops their weapon and puts both hands up. Bertil lets them go, minus their boots.
- The last of them goes down. Somebody laughs, too loudly.
- Isolde clears the dirt off a little shrine to Saint Oda and says a few words. They all rest their legs while it lasts. _(−10 fatigue each)_
- **They camp in Trevick's old office, with the portrait watching. Freya turns it to the wall.**
- The chaplain makes everyone eat and cleans every cut, and is the last one to sleep. _(1 food)_
- The watch shouts that they have company.
- The water on the gallery floor moves, and the cave crab climbs out of it claws first.
- Bertil shoots between two shoulders and hits the cave crab. _(4, it shrugs)_
- The cave crab twists away from the arming sword.
- Freya opens the cut on their palm again and casts from that. _(4 of their own)_
- Freya traces the figure without looking and the spell finds the cave crab. _(8)_
- Isolde waits behind the others for an opening that never comes.
- The cave crab rakes Kit and draws blood. _(17)_
- Kit slams a shoulder into the cave crab. _(8, it hates that)_

- Bertil goes clean through the cave crab and does not look surprised about it. _(7, it shrugs)_
- "Hold still," Isolde tells Kit, and pours something on the cut that makes Kit swear. _(+11)_
- Freya's little working dies before it reaches the cave crab.
- Kit gets the arming sword into the cave crab and twists. _(5, it shrugs)_
- A bolt from Bertil thumps into the cave crab. _(4, it shrugs)_
- Bertil finishes the cave crab with a blow behind the head.
- When it is done, somebody's hands will not stop shaking, and nobody mentions it.
- **At the end of the Trevick level Gaffer Moyle sits in a carved chair with a keg of powder beside it. He has been expecting somebody for six years.**
- Bertil shoots between two shoulders and hits the claim-jumper. _(8)_
- Gaffer Moyle gets in close and puts a knee into Bertil. _(10)_
- Kit catches Gaffer Moyle with the arming sword and something cracks. _(10)_
- Freya taps the claim-jumper with a working the size of a candle flame. _(5)_
- The powder man gets in close and lands a hard one on Bertil. _(18)_
- **Bertil crumples and somebody drags them back by the collar.**
- Isolde brings the flanged mace down on Gaffer Moyle. _(6)_
- Kit chops at Gaffer Moyle and connects. _(9)_
- Gaffer Moyle hits Isolde with a pick handle that has done this before. _(18)_
- Isolde packs their own wound and keeps going. _(+11)_
- Freya hits the claim-jumper so hard that the others stop to look. _(9)_
- The powder man swings at Kit and hits the air where Kit was standing.
- Kit shoves Gaffer Moyle back and off their footing. _(5)_
- Gaffer Moyle brings the pick handle down on Kit with six years of temper behind it. _(27)_
- Isolde kneels by Kit and packs the wound while the fight goes on around them. _(+11)_
- Freya pinches out a little working at the claim-jumper, about as much as they can spare, and it lands. _(5)_
- Freya knocks the claim-jumper senseless into the mud.
- The powder man kicks Kit in the knee and hits them while they are bent over. _(11)_
- Kit shoves Gaffer Moyle back and off their footing. _(4)_
- Gaffer Moyle goes down and is slow getting up.
- Isolde gets a pad of lint on Kit and leans on it until it stops. _(+11)_

- **They come home late and short of people. Jory meets them at the door and does not ask for the ledger yet.**
