# Drover's Road: sample expeditions

The cattle road through the hills east of the city, over Brannock Moor to the pass at Skell Gap.

Zones: the hill road, Brannock Moor, Skell Gap. Boss: take the toll house at Skell Gap from Sergeant Crewe.

Written by `npm run exp:logs`: a seasoned squad of four (level 3, rested) on a medium trip and on the long trip to the boss. Log lines as the player reads them; bold lines are the ones the game bolds.

## A Bull on the Moor: Kill a moor bull

_Medium route. Outcome: complete, 9 of 9 rooms, job done._


**Week 1**

- They leave Varrow at first light by the gate the carters use, and take the road to Drover's Road.

**Week 2**

- The road goes on. Jory counts milestones to pass the time, and gives up when one of them is missing.

**Week 3**

- They unpack, repack and set off into Drover's Road. Lise checks the knots twice.
- Hobb's people came through a few days ago. They took what was easy and left the gates open behind them.
- **The road narrows as it climbs. Below them the valley spreads out for miles with not a soul on it.**
- The road crosses a ford with the water up to their knees. The far bank is all mud.
- They come to a sheepfold beside the road, with a fire still smoking in one corner.
- Behind a loose stone in the wall Lise finds 2 purses of old coin, wrapped in sacking. _(2 purses of old coin)_
- Somebody signals, and they wait while the road agents go past.
- The first wolf trots out onto the road ahead, thin as a rake, and the rest of the pack comes after it.
- The second wolf closes its jaws on Jory's pack strap and comes away with the strap.
- The first wolf closes on Dace and will not let go. _(9)_
- The third wolf pushes at the ones in front and cannot get through.
- Dace doesn't have a clear shot and takes it anyway. It misses.
- Lise spends two doses at once. The plain burst fills the room. _(2 doses)_
- Lise lets go of the working. The first wolf is in the way of it. _(7)_
- Lise opens a hand and the focus spits at the second wolf. _(6)_
- Lise traces the figure without looking and the spell finds the third wolf. _(6)_
- Wat waits behind the others for an opening that never comes.
- Jory finds an opening and hits the second wolf in it. _(9)_
- The first wolf gets its teeth into Jory. _(5)_
- Jory slams a shoulder into the second wolf. _(6)_
- Jory finishes the second wolf with a blow behind the head.
- The third wolf catches Jory with a glancing blow that still hurts a great deal. _(4)_
- Dace's shot goes over the front rank and into the first wolf. _(15, it hates that)_
- The first wolf gives a last kick and lies still.
- Lise flicks a spark off the plain focus rod at the third wolf. It stings, at least. _(7)_
- Wat is too far back to hit anything, and knows it.
- Dace hits the third wolf so hard that the others stop to look. _(23, it hates that)_
- Dace puts the third wolf down at last.
- It goes quiet again, apart from something dripping.
- There are crossbows behind the rocks. "Purses on the road, please," says the first road agent.
- Dace shoots between two shoulders and hits the second road agent. _(7)_
- The second road agent clubs Wat across the side of the head. _(6)_
- The first road agent puts Jory on the ground and keeps hitting them. _(17)_
- Wat drags Jory back a yard and ties off the worst of the bleeding. _(+9)_
- Lise has nothing better left and throws a spark at the second road agent anyway. _(4)_
- Jory shoves the second road agent back and off their footing. _(4)_
- The second road agent reels and stands there with no idea where it is.
- The first road agent goes for Jory and gets tangled up with their own side.
- Jory chops at the second road agent and connects. _(10)_
- Jory puts the second road agent down, and the second road agent stays down.
- A bolt from Dace thumps into the first road agent. _(8)_
- Wat tries to push forward past the others and gets an elbow for it.
- Lise flicks a spark at the first road agent and misses.
- Dace looses, and the arrow takes the first road agent. _(7)_
- The first road agent twists away from the arming sword.
- Lise's little working dies before it reaches the first road agent.
- The first road agent kicks Lise in the knee and hits them while they are bent over. _(12)_
- Wat can't reach anything from back here and shouts advice instead.
- The first road agent gets a blade into Dace. _(7)_
- Dace picks a spot on the first road agent and puts an arrow in it. _(9)_
- The first road agent sits down hard and says they have had enough. Dace takes that as a yes.
- When it is done, somebody's hands will not stop shaking, and nobody mentions it.
- They eat standing up. Lise passes an onion round, which is generous of them, and nobody thanks them for it. _(1 food)_
- **They sleep in a ruined fold with a fire in the corner. The wind gets in through the gaps in the stones.**
- There is hot food, and the chaplain goes round everyone with a lamp and a needle. By morning they are in better shape than they deserve. _(1 food)_

**Week 4**

- **They come up onto the moor. The road is a line of stones across the heather, with a cairn every mile or so.**
- They come to a sheepfold of piled stone, with a gate made of a bed frame.
- Jory finds 2 purses of old coin caught on the heather where a flock went through. _(2 purses of old coin)_
- They reach a ring of old stones on a rise, with the whole moor laid out below.
- Dace finds 3 fleeces tied up in a bundle behind a wall, where a reiver meant to come back for it. _(3 fleeces)_
- Wat clears the dirt off a little shrine to Saint Oda and says a few words. They all rest their legs while it lasts. _(−10 fatigue each)_
- They share out bread and the end of a sausage. Wat makes theirs last as long as it can. _(1 food)_
- They reach the standing cairn the job named, in the middle of Brannock Moor. There are cattle penned behind it that do not belong to whoever is guarding them.
- There is a bull standing in the road. The moor bull lowers its head, and the horns go wide enough to take two of them at once.
- Jory batters the moor bull with the flat of the arming sword. _(3)_
- The moor bull goes down and is slow getting up.
- The air goes tight around Lise, and then the moor bull takes the spell full on. _(10)_
- Dace looses, and the arrow takes the moor bull. _(12, it hates that)_
- Wat can't reach anything from back here and shouts advice instead.
- The moor bull bites Jory on the arm and shakes. _(24)_
- Jory catches the moor bull with the arming sword and something cracks. _(8)_
- Dace's shot goes over the front rank and into the moor bull. _(12, it hates that)_
- The moor bull limps off a few yards and falls over.
- They wipe the blades on whatever is nearest and count the bandages.
- Dace goes through the reivers' camp by the cairn and comes back with 4 fleeces. _(4 fleeces)_
- The packs are full. They turn round and start back out of Drover's Road, watching both sides of the path.

**Week 5**

- **They are home. The hall smells of tallow and wet dog, and Jory sits down in the doorway and takes their boots off where they are.**

_Home: nobody lost. Deeds: Completed a medium expedition (+2 renown)._

## The Toll House at Skell Gap: Take the toll house at Skell Gap from Sergeant Crewe

_Long route. Outcome: complete, 14 of 14 rooms, job done._


**Week 1**

- The squad sets out for Drover's Road with the packs on their backs. Tam has started complaining about the straps before the end of Tallow Street.

**Week 2**

- They go into Drover's Road with as much as they can carry and a map that Isolde bought in Varrow and now suspects.
- **The Drover's Road climbs out of the valley between stone walls. There are sheep on both sides and nobody else.**
- Grey shapes keep pace with them along the wall. The wolves have been following since the last farm.
- The second wolf closes its jaws on Sabine's pack strap and comes away with the strap.
- The first wolf rakes Tam and draws blood. _(7)_
- Sabine doesn't have a clear shot and takes it anyway. It misses.
- Isolde cracks two doses together and the plain working goes off across the whole front. _(2 doses)_
- Isolde lets go of the working. The first wolf is in the way of it. _(6)_
- Isolde traces the figure without looking and the spell finds the second wolf. _(6)_
- Isolde opens a hand and the focus spits at the third wolf. _(6)_
- Petra tries to push forward past the others and gets an elbow for it.
- Tam slams a shoulder into the first wolf. _(7)_
- The first wolf goes down and is slow getting up.
- Sabine looses, and the arrow takes the first wolf. _(17, it hates that)_
- The first wolf limps off a few yards and falls over.
- The second wolf catches Tam with a glancing blow that still hurts a great deal. _(9)_
- Isolde flicks a spark off the plain focus rod at the second wolf. It stings, at least. _(5)_
- Petra can't reach anything from back here and shouts advice instead.
- Tam swings at the second wolf and hits the dirt.
- The second wolf slams into Tam from the side. _(9)_
- Sabine gets out of the way of the third wolf.
- Tam steps in and hits the second wolf hard with the arming sword. _(10)_
- Tam puts the second wolf down at last.
- Sabine's shot goes over the front rank and into the third wolf. _(15, it hates that)_
- Sabine finishes the third wolf with a blow behind the head.
- It is over. They stand there breathing hard and counting each other.
- There are crossbows behind the rocks. "Purses on the road, please," says the first road agent.
- The second road agent gets in close and lands a hard one on Tam. _(12)_
- Tam chops at the second road agent and connects. _(9)_
- Sabine shoots, and the second road agent moves at the last moment.
- The first road agent kicks Petra in the knee and hits them while they are bent over. _(13)_
- Isolde pinches out a little working at the second road agent, about as much as they can spare, and it lands. _(4)_
- Petra stitches Tam up in three movements, with big crooked stitches that hold anyway. _(+9)_
- Sabine shoots between two shoulders and hits the second road agent. _(8)_
- The second road agent drops their weapon and puts both hands up. Sabine lets them go, minus their boots.
- Isolde flicks a small working at the first road agent, the cheapest one they know. _(6)_
- The first road agent feints at Tam and then forgets to follow it up.
- Tam batters the first road agent with the flat of the arming sword. _(6)_
- Petra gets a dressing on Tam, tight and fast. _(+9)_
- A bolt from Sabine thumps into the first road agent. _(9)_
- Sabine knocks the first road agent senseless into the mud.
- It goes quiet again, apart from something dripping.
- They come to a sheepfold beside the road, with a fire still smoking in one corner.
- Isolde finds 3 hides on a drover who will not be needing it, and says a word over him. _(3 hides)_
- **The road climbs out of the valley onto Brannock Moor. It is heather and standing water as far as anyone can see, and the wind comes from all sides.**
- They leave the path and give the moor bulls a wide berth.
- Petra clears the dirt off a little shrine to Saint Oda and says a few words. They all rest their legs while it lasts. _(−10 fatigue each)_
- They stop and eat cold bread and hard cheese. Isolde says it is the best meal of their life. _(1 food)_
- Sabine sees the herd start to move and waves the squad up onto a rock. The cattle go past on both sides. _(1 tools)_

**Week 3**

- They hear reivers coming and get down out of sight until the noise has gone by.
- They reach a ring of old stones on a rise, with the whole moor laid out below.
- Petra finds a fleece caught on the heather where a flock went through. _(a fleece)_
- The moor bull lifts its head out of the heather. It is a red, shaggy animal the size of a hay cart, and it does not like the look of anybody.
- Isolde gets the blow they were waiting for. The moor bull feels all of it. _(20)_
- Tam shoves the moor bull back and off their footing. _(4)_
- Sabine picks a spot on the moor bull and puts an arrow in it. _(11, it hates that)_
- Petra waits behind the others for an opening that never comes.
- The moor bull gets its teeth into Tam. _(21)_
- The moor bull lunges, and Sabine sidesteps it.
- The air goes tight around Isolde, and then the moor bull takes the spell full on. _(9)_
- Sabine looses, and the arrow takes the moor bull. _(14, it hates that)_
- The moor bull gives a last kick and lies still.
- That is the end of it. Everybody checks their hands for missing fingers.
- Petra takes a bandage to Tam and makes it go twice as far as anyone else would. _(+16)_
- **They make camp in a shepherd's hut. The shepherd isn't in, and he has left a note saying not to eat the cheese.**
- There is hot food, and the chaplain goes round everyone with a lamp and a needle. By morning they are in better shape than they deserve. _(1 food)_
- **They climb into the pass. The wind comes through it hard, and the slopes on either side are grey and loose.**
- They reach an old Crown milestone with the distances to Varrow chiselled off and something else written over them.
- Sabine goes through an empty tent and comes out with 2 fleeces. _(2 fleeces)_
- They come to a stretch of the road where the slopes close in. Somebody has piled stones across it to make a wall.
- Isolde finds 4 purses of old coin in a soldier's pack left by the road. _(4 purses of old coin)_

**Week 4**

- The road opens into a hollow where the drovers used to rest their herds. There are tents in it.
- Under the cairn with the helmet on it, Petra finds 3 purses of old coin. _(3 purses of old coin)_
- They eat sitting on the packs with the wind in their faces. Nobody talks much. _(1 food)_
- There is a bull standing in the road. The moor bull lowers its head, and the horns go wide enough to take two of them at once.
- Sabine knocks the blow aside.
- Sabine's arrow goes over the reiver and into the distance.
- Tam times it perfectly and the reiver pays for it. _(15)_
- Isolde speaks the working and it hits the reiver square. _(11)_
- The reiver takes one look at how the fight is going and runs for it.
- Tam jabs at the moor bull, and it backs off.
- Petra is too far back to hit anything, and knows it.
- Sabine finds the weak place on the moor bull, and it goes in deep. _(25, it hates that)_
- Isolde speaks the working and it hits the moor bull square. _(11)_
- Petra waits behind the others for an opening that never comes.
- The moor bull snaps at Tam and gets nothing.
- Tam slams a shoulder into the moor bull. _(6)_
- Isolde has nothing better left and throws a spark at the moor bull anyway. _(3)_
- Sabine's shot goes over the front rank and into the moor bull. _(14, it hates that)_
- Sabine gets the moor bull in the right place, and it drops.
- They wipe the blades on whatever is nearest and count the bandages.
- **They camp in the lee of a boulder in the pass, with the wind going over the top of it all night.**
- The chaplain makes everyone eat and cleans every cut, and is the last one to sleep. _(1 food)_
- **Sergeant Crewe is waiting at the toll house with a pike and twenty years in the Ninth. "Toll's a crown a head," he says. "Or you can turn round."**
- Isolde pays for the next working in blood. Their lips have gone blue. _(4 of their own)_
- Isolde hits the deserter so hard that the others stop to look. _(14)_
- Sabine goes clean through the deserter and does not look surprised about it. _(17)_
- Sergeant Crewe gets his pike through Sabine's guard with a thrust straight off the drill square. _(16)_
- The deserter lunges at Tam and overreaches.
- Petra tries to push forward past the others and gets an elbow for it.
- Tam shoves the deserter back and off their footing. _(4)_
- Tam gets the deserter on the ground, and the deserter decides that is where they will stay.
- Isolde opens the cut on their palm again and casts from that. _(4 of their own)_
- Isolde lets go of the working. Sergeant Crewe is in the way of it. _(9)_
- Tam knocks the pike aside, and Sergeant Crewe steps back into line.
- Sabine shoots between two shoulders and hits Sergeant Crewe. _(9)_
- Petra is too far back to hit anything, and knows it.
- Tam batters Sergeant Crewe with the flat of the arming sword. _(4)_
- Isolde casts dry. It comes out of them instead of the doses, and it hurts. _(4 of their own)_
- The air goes tight around Isolde, and then Sergeant Crewe takes the spell full on. _(8)_
- The wind takes Sabine's arrow wide of Sergeant Crewe.
- Sergeant Crewe thrusts at Sabine and misses, and swears at himself the way he used to swear at recruits.
- Tam charges Sergeant Crewe and finds only air.
- Petra can't reach anything from back here and shouts advice instead.
- Sergeant Crewe hits Tam with the butt of his pike and then with the point. _(11)_
- Sabine picks a spot on Sergeant Crewe and puts an arrow in it. _(8)_
- Isolde has run out of reagents. They spend their own blood instead, and go grey. _(4 of their own)_
- Isolde opens a hand and the focus spits at Sergeant Crewe. _(9)_
- Tam puts their weight behind the arming sword, and Sergeant Crewe takes all of it. _(8)_
- Petra drags Isolde back a yard and ties off the worst of the bleeding. _(+9)_
- Tam lunges, slips in the mud and misses Sergeant Crewe.
- The wind takes Sabine's arrow wide of Sergeant Crewe.
- There are no doses left, so Isolde cuts their own palm and casts from that. _(4 of their own)_
- Isolde traces the figure without looking and the spell finds Sergeant Crewe. _(9)_
- Sergeant Crewe steps in and catches Tam under the arm. _(12)_
- Petra gets a pad of lint on Tam and leans on it until it stops. _(+9)_
- Sergeant Crewe gets his pike through Tam's guard with a thrust straight off the drill square. _(17)_
- A bolt from Sabine thumps into Sergeant Crewe. _(8)_
- Tam gets the arming sword into Sergeant Crewe and twists. _(10)_
- Isolde flicks a spark at Sergeant Crewe and misses.
- Petra kneels by Tam and packs the wound while the fight goes on around them. _(+9)_
- Isolde taps Sergeant Crewe with a working the size of a candle flame. _(4)_
- A bolt from Sabine thumps into Sergeant Crewe. _(8)_
- Tam catches Sergeant Crewe with the arming sword and something cracks. _(10)_
- Sergeant Crewe drops his pike and sits on the toll house step. "That's that, then," he says, and he doesn't get up.
- It is over. The deserters are walking down the far side of the pass, and none of them looks back.
- Behind the barrier Isolde finds 4 purses of old coin, the takings of a season's tolls. _(4 purses of old coin)_
- The packs are full. They turn round and start back out of Drover's Road, watching both sides of the path.

**Week 5**

- **They are home. The hall smells of tallow and wet dog, and Sabine sits down in the doorway and takes their boots off where they are.**

_Home: nobody lost. Deeds: Completed a long expedition (+4 renown); Took the toll house at Skell Gap (+8 renown)._
