# The Saltfens: sample expeditions

The marshes along the estuary, good for reagents and salt and for catching fever.

Zones: the reed paths, the Pans, the Withies. Boss: kill the Dean.

Written by `npm run exp:logs`: a seasoned squad of four (level 3, rested) on a medium trip and on the long trip to the boss. Log lines as the player reads them; bold lines are the ones the game bolds.

## Salt for the Wounds: Bring back 4 cakes of pan salt

_Medium route. Outcome: complete, 9 of 9 rooms, job done._


**Week 1**

- Tam walks at the front for the first mile, until the novelty wears off. It is a long way to The Saltfens.

**Week 2**

- They sleep in a barn one night and under a hedge the next. The Saltfens is still some way off.

**Week 3**

- They unpack, repack and set off into The Saltfens. Tam checks the knots twice.
- Hobb's people came through a few days ago. They took what was easy and left the gates open behind them.
- **The path goes into the Saltfens on planks laid end to end. The reeds close over their heads within twenty yards.**
- People come out of the reeds with salt rakes in their hands. They have been out on the pans too long, and they do not look well.
- Ulla chops at the first salt-cutter and connects. _(7)_
- Ulla comes back the other way and catches the first salt-cutter. _(5)_
- Tam shoots between two shoulders and hits the first salt-cutter. _(10)_
- Tam knocks the first salt-cutter senseless into the mud.
- Kester shoves the second salt-cutter back and off their footing. _(7)_
- The second salt-cutter lurches and loses track of the fight.
- Osric looses, and the arrow takes the second salt-cutter. _(8)_
- Tam picks a spot on the second salt-cutter and puts an arrow in it. _(9)_
- The second salt-cutter sits down hard and says they have had enough. Tam takes that as a yes.
- They wipe the blades on whatever is nearest and count the bandages.
- There is a drying frame here, hung with plants an apothecary in Varrow would pay for.
- Ulla cuts a bundle of reagents from the edge of the pans, up to the elbows in brine. _(a bundle of reagents)_
- The reeds open onto a salt pan that glares white in the sun. They cross it fast with their eyes half shut.
- While the others rest, Osric goes through a heap of junk by the path and comes back with a bundle of reagents. _(a bundle of reagents)_
- They pass a cutter's hut on stilts. There is a pot on the stove inside and nobody home.
- They eat sitting on the packs with the wind in their faces. Nobody talks much. _(1 food)_
- **They camp in a cutter's hut on stilts. It sways whenever anyone turns over.**
- They eat and sleep in turns. Ulla takes the first watch and does not complain about it. _(1 food)_

**Week 4**

- **The reeds stop and the Pans begin. There are low mud walls around square ponds of brine, with a white crust over everything, and nothing at all between the squad and the sky.**
- Osric sees that the crust ahead is too thin to stand on, and lays a plank across before anyone tries. _(1 tools)_
- Kester looks down. The first knot of leeches has come up out of the channel and is going up their boots.
- Tam hits the salt-cutter so hard that the others stop to look. _(15)_
- The wind takes Osric's arrow wide of the salt-cutter.
- Kester steps in and hits the second knot of leeches hard with the arming sword. _(10)_
- The second knot of leeches limps off a few yards and falls over.
- Ulla swings at the salt-cutter and hits the dirt.
- Ulla's second strike finds the salt-cutter. _(6)_
- Ulla puts the salt-cutter down, and the salt-cutter stays down.
- The first knot of leeches goes for Kester and overshoots.
- Tam's arrow goes over the first knot of leeches and into the distance.
- Ulla swings the spear into the first knot of leeches and keeps swinging. _(6)_
- Ulla follows through and opens the first knot of leeches up a second time. _(6)_
- Ulla gets the first knot of leeches in the right place, and it drops.
- They win that one. Nobody says anything for a while.
- There is an empty boathouse here. Tam goes through it and finds a purse and a sack of food somebody stowed in the rafters. _(a purse of coin, 1 food)_
- They eat standing up. Osric passes an onion round, which is generous of them, and nobody thanks them for it. _(1 food)_
- The boiling house is at the end of the last pan wall. People are standing in its door, and they have the look the fever gives them.
- The reeds shake, and a black hog comes through them at a run.
- Ulla finds an opening and hits the salt-cutter in it. _(6)_
- Ulla does not wait to see what the first one did, and hits the salt-cutter again. _(7)_
- Osric's shot goes over the front rank and into the salt-cutter. _(9)_
- The salt-cutter drops their weapon and puts both hands up. Osric lets them go, minus their boots.
- A bolt from Tam thumps into the marsh hog. _(15, it hates that)_
- The marsh hog snaps at Ulla and gets nothing.
- Kester misses, and says something the chaplains would not repeat.
- Ulla catches the marsh hog with the spear and something cracks. _(7, it hates that)_
- Ulla goes for a second cut, and the marsh hog is ready for that one.
- Tam's shot goes over the front rank and into the marsh hog. _(15, it hates that)_
- Osric looses, and the arrow takes the marsh hog. _(15, it hates that)_
- Osric puts the marsh hog down at last.
- It is over. They stand there breathing hard and counting each other.
- In the drying loft of the boiling house Ulla finds 5 cakes of pan salt, stacked to the rafters. _(5 cakes of pan salt)_
- The packs are full. They turn round and start back out of The Saltfens, watching both sides of the path.

**Week 5**

- It is another week on the road home. Ulla keeps checking the packs to make sure the haul is still in them.

**Week 6**

- **They come in through the gate at dusk and up Tallow Street with the haul. Jory comes to the hall door with the ledger already open.**

_Home: nobody lost. Deeds: Completed a medium expedition (+2 renown)._
