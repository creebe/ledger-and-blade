# The Saltfens: sample expeditions

The marshes along the estuary, good for reagents and salt and for catching fever.

Zones: the reed paths, the Pans, the Withies. Boss: kill the Dean.

Written by `npm run exp:logs`: a seasoned squad of four (level 3, rested) on a medium trip and on the long trip to the boss. Log lines as the player reads them; bold lines are the ones the game bolds.

## Pan Salt: Bring back 4 cakes of pan salt

_Medium route. Outcome: complete, 9 of 9 rooms, job done._


**Week 1**

- Tam walks at the front for the first mile, until the novelty wears off. It is a long way to The Saltfens.

**Week 2**

- They sleep in a barn one night and under a hedge the next. The Saltfens is still some way off.

**Week 3**

- They unpack, repack and set off into The Saltfens. Tam checks the knots twice.
- **The road gives out at a cutter's hut. After that it is planks and reeds and the smell of the estuary.**
- Something snorts in the reeds close by. The marsh hog has smelled them and does not like it.
- Ulla slashes at the salt-cutter and leaves it bleeding. _(5)_
- Osric's shot goes over the front rank and into the salt-cutter. _(8)_
- The salt-cutter gives ground, and Kester stumbles past it.
- Tam finds the weak place on the marsh hog, and it goes in deep. _(14, it hates that)_
- The marsh hog lunges, and Kester sidesteps it.
- The salt-cutter is leaving a trail of blood. _(3)_
- The salt-cutter catches Kester with a knife on the backswing. _(7)_
- Ulla finds an opening and hits the salt-cutter in it. _(5)_
- Ulla knocks the salt-cutter senseless into the mud.
- Ulla cuts again, faster, and finds the marsh hog. _(9, it hates that)_
- Osric shoots between two shoulders and hits the marsh hog. _(12, it hates that)_
- Osric finishes the marsh hog with a blow behind the head.
- The last of them goes down. Somebody laughs, too loudly.
- The path ends at an old salt-house, half sunk, with its door hanging open.
- Osric wades out and comes back with 3 bundles of reagents and a leech on each leg. _(3 bundles of reagents)_
- They try to wait it out, and the marsh hogs find them anyway.
- The reeds shake, and a black hog comes through them at a run.
- **They walk straight into it. The marsh hog has the first move.**
- The salt-cutter kicks Ulla in the knee and hits them while they are bent over. _(12)_
- The marsh hog bites Kester on the arm and shakes. _(19)_
- Ulla goes low and opens a long cut in the salt-cutter. _(6)_
- Tam puts an arrow into the marsh hog, and it won't be aiming at anyone for a while. _(6, it hates that)_
- Kester slams a shoulder into the salt-cutter. _(5)_
- A bolt from Osric thumps into the salt-cutter. _(9)_
- The salt-cutter drops their weapon and puts both hands up. Osric lets them go, minus their boots.
- Ulla cuts the marsh hog somewhere that will keep bleeding. _(7, it hates that)_
- Osric looses, and the arrow takes the marsh hog. _(15, it hates that)_
- Tam picks a spot on the marsh hog and puts an arrow in it. _(12, it hates that)_
- The marsh hog limps off a few yards and falls over.
- That is the end of it. Everybody checks their hands for missing fingers.
- Something big moves in the reeds off to one side and keeps pace with them for a while, then stops.
- The reeds open onto a salt pan that glares white in the sun. They cross it fast with their eyes half shut.
- They eat standing up. Osric passes an onion round, which is generous of them, and nobody thanks them for it. _(1 food)_
- **They camp in a cutter's hut on stilts. It sways whenever anyone turns over.**
- They get a small fire going under a pot. Wounds get looked at, and people sleep where they sit. _(1 food)_

**Week 4**

- **They come out onto the salt pans. The light is flat and white, and Kester has to squint to see the far wall.**
- Osric prods the path ahead with a pole and finds no bottom. They lay planks across, and everyone keeps their boots. _(1 tools)_
- Something snorts in the reeds close by. The first marsh hog has smelled them and does not like it.
- Ulla brings the spear down on the first marsh hog. _(7, it hates that)_
- Ulla follows through and opens the first marsh hog up a second time. _(10, it hates that)_
- Kester batters the first marsh hog with the flat of the arming sword. _(6)_
- The first marsh hog reels and stands there with no idea where it is.
- A bolt from Tam thumps into the first marsh hog. _(12, it hates that)_
- Osric shoots, and the first marsh hog moves at the last moment.
- The second marsh hog snaps at Kester and gets nothing.
- Ulla swings the spear into the first marsh hog and keeps swinging. _(9, it hates that)_
- The first marsh hog gives a last kick and lies still.
- Ulla does not wait to see what the first one did, and hits the second marsh hog again. _(10, it hates that)_
- Tam's arrow goes over the second marsh hog and into the distance.
- Osric's shot goes over the front rank and into the second marsh hog. _(12, it hates that)_
- The second marsh hog catches Kester with a glancing blow that still hurts a great deal. _(31)_
- **Kester drops, then sits up again with the empty draught bottle still in their mouth.** _(a draught)_
- Kester shoves the second marsh hog back and off their footing. _(6)_
- The second marsh hog lurches and loses track of the fight.
- Ulla steps in and hits the second marsh hog hard with the spear. _(10, it hates that)_
- Ulla gets the second marsh hog in the right place, and it drops.
- They win that one. Nobody says anything for a while.
- Kester binds their own cuts with a fresh bandage. _(+8)_
- They keep still in cover, and the salt-cutters go by close enough to smell.
- Ulla finds a little shrine to Saint Oda, older than the hall in Varrow. They stop for a breather. _(−4 fatigue each)_
- They eat sitting on the packs with the wind in their faces. Nobody talks much. _(1 food)_
- The boiling house is at the end of the last pan wall. People are standing in its door, and they have the look the fever gives them.
- The water at the edge of the channel starts to move, and the first knot of leeches comes out of it.
- The wind takes Osric's arrow wide of the second knot of leeches.
- The second knot of leeches twists away from the spear.
- Ulla comes back the other way and catches the first knot of leeches. _(6)_
- Ulla jabs at the first knot of leeches, and it backs off.
- The second knot of leeches gets its teeth into Kester. _(4)_
- Kester batters the first knot of leeches with the flat of the arming sword. _(4, it shrugs)_
- Kester puts the first knot of leeches down at last.
- Tam looses, and the arrow takes the second knot of leeches. _(10)_
- The second knot of leeches gives a last kick and lies still.
- Osric shoots between two shoulders and hits the salt-cutter. _(8)_
- Tam pins the salt-cutter where it stands. _(6)_
- Ulla misses, and says something the chaplains would not repeat.
- Ulla tries a second strike and nearly loses the spear.
- Kester knocks the blow aside.
- Kester gets the arming sword into the salt-cutter and twists. _(11)_
- Kester puts the salt-cutter down, and the salt-cutter stays down.
- It is over. They stand there breathing hard and counting each other.
- In the drying loft of the boiling house Ulla finds 4 cakes of pan salt, stacked to the rafters. _(4 cakes of pan salt)_
- The packs are full. They turn round and start back out of The Saltfens, watching both sides of the path.

**Week 5**

- It is another week on the road home. Kester keeps checking the packs to make sure the haul is still in them.

**Week 6**

- **The squad walks back into Varrow footsore, with the job done. Kester goes straight to the Copper Kettle and is not seen again until morning.**

_Home: nobody lost. Deeds: Completed a medium expedition (+2 renown); Fought off a wandering pack (+1 renown)._

## A Boar in the Withies: Kill the Dean

_Long route. Outcome: complete, 14 of 14 rooms, job done._


**Week 1**

- They leave Varrow at first light by the gate the carters use, and take the road to The Saltfens.

**Week 2**

- The road goes on. Tam counts milestones to pass the time, and gives up when one of them is missing.

**Week 3**

- Elric takes the first step into The Saltfens. The farmer who gave them a bed last night told them not to go, and said it twice.
- **They are in the reeds now. The water under the planks is brown and warm, and the flies find them at once.**
- There is a drying frame here, hung with plants an apothecary in Varrow would pay for.
- Tam finds 3 bundles of reagents in a cutter's lean-to, dried and tied up for market. _(3 bundles of reagents)_
- They try to wait it out, and the salt-cutters find them anyway.
- People come out of the reeds with salt rakes in their hands. They have been out on the pans too long, and they do not look well.
- **The first salt-cutter gets the first blow in before anyone sees it coming.**
- The first salt-cutter gets in close and lands a hard one on Elric. _(5)_
- The second salt-cutter clubs Bertil across the side of the head. _(10)_
- Bertil looses, and the arrow takes the second salt-cutter. _(8)_
- Elric slams a shoulder into the second salt-cutter. _(7)_
- The second salt-cutter reels and stands there with no idea where it is.
- Tam flicks a spark at the second salt-cutter and misses.
- Marten is too far back to hit anything, and knows it.
- Bertil hits the second salt-cutter so hard that the others stop to look. _(14)_
- Bertil gets the second salt-cutter on the ground, and the second salt-cutter decides that is where they will stay.
- The first salt-cutter drives the point into Elric's shoulder. _(10)_
- Marten can't reach anything from back here and shouts advice instead.
- Tam's little working dies before it reaches the first salt-cutter.
- Elric gets the arming sword into the first salt-cutter and twists. _(10)_
- Bertil times it perfectly and the first salt-cutter pays for it. _(17)_
- Bertil puts the first salt-cutter down, and the first salt-cutter stays down.
- They win that one. Nobody says anything for a while.
- They come out on a hummock of dry ground with a hut on it and a boat pulled up.
- Bertil cuts a bundle of reagents from the edge of the pans, up to the elbows in brine. _(a bundle of reagents)_
- They pass a cutter's hut on stilts. There is a pot on the stove inside and nobody home.
- **They come out onto the salt pans. The light is flat and white, and Elric has to squint to see the far wall.**
- The fowler rises out of a hide with a punt gun as long as a man and points it at the squad, which is a lot of gun for a duck.
- Bertil picks a spot on the salt-cutter and puts an arrow in it. _(9)_
- The salt-cutter hits Bertil with everything they have, and Bertil folds up round it. _(14)_
- The fowler gets a blade into Marten. _(15)_
- Elric brings the arming sword down on the salt-cutter. _(10)_
- Tam finds the weak place on the salt-cutter, and it goes in deep. _(15)_
- The salt-cutter sits down hard and says they have had enough. Tam takes that as a yes.
- Marten drags Bertil back a yard and ties off the worst of the bleeding. _(+9)_
- Bertil shoots between two shoulders and hits the fowler. _(10)_
- Tam pinches out a little working at the fowler, about as much as they can spare, and it lands. _(3)_
- The fowler kicks Elric in the knee and hits them while they are bent over. _(14)_
- Elric puts their weight behind the arming sword, and the fowler takes all of it. _(9)_
- Elric knocks the fowler senseless into the mud.
- The last of them goes down. Somebody laughs, too loudly.
- They stop and eat cold bread and hard cheese. Tam says it is the best meal of their life. _(1 food)_
- Marten sits Elric down on a step and does the job properly. _(+16)_
- Tam licks a finger and touches the crust, and then wishes they hadn't.

**Week 4**

- Bertil spots a soft patch in the crust where the brine is just underneath, and they all step round it. _(1 tools)_
- Somebody's buckle clinks, and the fen leeches turn round.
- The water at the edge of the channel starts to move, and the knot of leeches comes out of it.
- **Nobody was watching that side. The knot of leeches is already among them.**
- The knot of leeches catches Elric with a glancing blow that still hurts a great deal. _(4)_
- Tam goes clean through the knot of leeches and does not look surprised about it. _(9)_
- Tam finishes the knot of leeches with a blow behind the head.
- That is the end of it. Everybody checks their hands for missing fingers.
- They walk along the top of a pan wall with brine on both sides. The crust crunches under their boots.
- Bertil notices a hollow under a tree root with a cake of pan salt stuffed into it. _(a cake of pan salt)_
- **They make camp in the lee of a pan wall, out of the wind. Elric keeps watch and watches the stars, because there is nothing else to watch.**
- There is hot food, and the chaplain goes round everyone with a lamp and a needle. By morning they are in better shape than they deserve. _(1 food)_
- **The willows close in around them. The water in the channels is brown and quick, and nobody wants to fall in.**
- Bertil finds a wire noose at ankle height and cuts it before anyone puts a foot in it. _(1 tools)_
- They keep still in cover, and the fen leeches go by close enough to smell.
- "These are my beds," says the first fowler, coming out of the willows with a fowling gun. "Go and shoot your own ducks."
- A bolt from Bertil thumps into the second fowler. _(10)_
- The first fowler catches Bertil with a knife on the backswing. _(15)_
- Elric batters the second fowler with the flat of the arming sword. _(6)_
- "Hold still," Marten tells Bertil, and pours something on the cut that makes Bertil swear. _(+9)_
- Tam has nothing better left and throws a spark at the second fowler anyway. _(5)_
- The second fowler drops their weapon and puts both hands up. Tam lets them go, minus their boots.
- Elric steps in and hits the first fowler hard with the arming sword. _(9)_
- Bertil's shot goes over the front rank and into the first fowler. _(8)_
- Tam flicks a small working at the first fowler, the cheapest one they know. _(3)_
- The first fowler takes one look at how the fight is going and runs for it.
- When it is done, somebody's hands will not stop shaking, and nobody mentions it.

**Week 5**

- They try to slip past the marsh hogs and get caught in the open.
- Something snorts in the reeds close by. The marsh hog has smelled them and does not like it.
- **They walk straight into it. The marsh hog has the first move.**
- Elric gets out of the way of the marsh hog.
- A bolt from Bertil thumps into the marsh hog. _(12, it hates that)_

- Bertil gets the blow they were waiting for. The marsh hog feels all of it. _(23, it hates that)_
- Bertil gets the marsh hog in the right place, and it drops.
- It is over. They stand there breathing hard and counting each other.
- The path comes out at a punt landing with a rope across the channel and no punt.
- In a fowler's hide, under a heap of decoys, Elric finds 3 bundles of reagents. _(3 bundles of reagents)_
- They eat sitting on the packs with the wind in their faces. Nobody talks much. _(1 food)_
- They reach an island of solid ground, with a fowler's hide on it built of reeds and mud.
- Elric pulls 2 bundles of reagents out of the mud at the edge of the channel, where somebody dropped it. _(2 bundles of reagents)_
- **They make camp in a fowler's hide, close together because there is no other way. Elric snores.**
- The chaplain makes everyone eat and cleans every cut, and is the last one to sleep. _(1 food)_
- Something finds the camp in the night.
- The reeds shake, and a black hog comes through them at a run.
- Tam lets go of the working. The second marsh hog is in the way of it. _(9)_
- The first marsh hog slams into Elric from the side. _(34)_
- Bertil picks a spot on the second marsh hog and puts an arrow in it. _(12, it hates that)_

- Bertil's shot goes over the front rank and into the second marsh hog. _(12, it hates that)_
- Bertil puts the second marsh hog down at last.
- Marten gets a dressing on Elric, tight and fast. _(+9)_
- The first marsh hog knocks Elric flat and goes for them on the ground. _(16)_
- Elric shoves the first marsh hog back and off their footing. _(6)_
- The first marsh hog lurches and loses track of the fight.
- Bertil gets the blow they were waiting for. The first marsh hog feels all of it. _(25, it hates that)_
- Marten gets a pad of lint on Elric and leans on it until it stops. _(+9)_
- Tam opens a hand and the focus spits at the first marsh hog. _(11)_
- Elric slams a shoulder into the first marsh hog. _(6)_
- The first marsh hog gives a last kick and lies still.
- It goes quiet again, apart from something dripping.
- **The Dean is lying in the wallow at the end of the Withies. It gets up, and it keeps getting up. It is the size of a cart.**
- Bertil looses, and the arrow takes the marsh hog. _(13, it hates that)_
- Marten kneels by Elric and packs the wound while the fight goes on around them. _(+9)_
- The Dean charges at Bertil and goes past into the willows, taking three of them with it.
- Tam has run out of reagents. They spend their own blood instead, and go grey. _(4 of their own)_

- Elric batters the marsh hog with the flat of the arming sword. _(6)_

- Bertil shoots between two shoulders and hits the marsh hog. _(15, it hates that)_

- Elric shoves the Dean back and off their footing. _(4)_
