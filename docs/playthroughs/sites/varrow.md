# Varrow: sample expeditions

Work in the city itself, where the trouble is guarded doors and angry crowds, and further down the hill, people who rent out both.

Zones: the Lower Town, the Quays, the Rookeries. Boss: put Lew Rudd out of business.

Written by `npm run exp:logs`: a seasoned squad of four (level 3, rested) on a medium trip and on the long trip to the boss. Log lines as the player reads them; bold lines are the ones the game bolds.

## Duty Unpaid: Bring back 3 casks of bonded brandy

_Medium route. Outcome: complete, 9 of 9 rooms, job done._


**Week 1**

- They go out past the green, where somebody is selling hot chestnuts. Quentin buys a bag for the road, and it is gone by the first milestone.

**Week 2**

- It is another week on the road. Godwin has worked out which boot is letting the water in, and it is both of them.

**Week 3**

- A carter going the other way tells them it is not far now. He has probably said that to everybody for twenty years.

**Week 4**

- They take two weeks' food and a list of addresses in Jory's hand. Cora reads it twice and puts it in a boot.
- **They go down the hill into the Lower Town, where the streets narrow and the gutters are full.**
- There is a counting-house at the end of the lane, shuttered for the night, with a light still on upstairs.
- Cora finds a purse of old coin in a drawer that somebody forgot to lock. _(a purse of old coin)_
- The first thug cracks their knuckles. It is meant to be frightening, and it mostly works.
- Cora steps forward with empty hands and a story. Whether the first thug believes it or not, the whole lot of them stand aside.
- Somebody signals, and they lose themselves in the crowd while the guards go past.
- They climb a set of back stairs that smell of cabbage. A door opens on the landing and shuts again.
- They cut through a cooper's yard. The cooper watches them all the way across and does not put down the mallet.
- They eat standing up in an alley. Godwin passes an onion round, which is generous of them, and nobody thanks them for it. _(1 food)_
- **They hole up for the night in a loft over a stable and pull the ladder up after them.**
- They eat hot food for the first time in days. Godwin sleeps sitting up with the spoon still in hand. _(1 food)_

**Week 5**

- **They come down the river stairs onto the Quays after dark. The lighters are tied up three deep, and the warehouses have men in the doorways who do not look like warehousemen.**
- The lamps go up, and a dog starts barking two streets over and keeps at it.
- The quay opens out into a timber yard, with stacks of planks higher than a house and paths between them.
- Ursel finds 3 casks of bonded brandy behind a stack of crates, put there by someone who meant to come back for it. _(3 casks of bonded brandy)_
- There is a weighbridge here, and a hut beside it with a light on and nobody inside.
- Godwin prises the lid off a crate marked 'NAILS' and finds 2 purses of old coin. _(2 purses of old coin)_
- They walk along the quay edge between bollards. The river slaps at the steps below, and Godwin keeps well away from it.
- While the others rest, Godwin goes through a heap of rubble and comes back with a cask of bonded brandy. _(a cask of bonded brandy)_
- They buy hot pies from a stall and eat them walking. Cora burns their mouth and keeps going. _(1 food)_
- The bonded warehouse is at the end of the Quays, with a lamp over the door and people under it.
- The first lighterman comes along the quay with a hook over one shoulder. "Nobody walks here after dark," they say, "except us."
- Cora offers the first lighterman a way out. The first lighterman laughs, and that is the end of the talking.
- Quentin cuts the first lighterman somewhere that will keep bleeding. _(5)_
- The second lighterman clubs Ursel across the side of the head. _(13)_
- A bolt from Godwin thumps into the first lighterman. _(8)_
- Ursel batters the first lighterman with the flat of the arming sword. _(5)_
- The first lighterman lurches and loses track of the fight.
- Cora points out to the second lighterman that the others have left them at the front to die. The second lighterman looks round to check.
- The first lighterman is bleeding badly. _(3)_
- Godwin's shot goes over the front rank and into the first lighterman. _(10)_
- Godwin knocks the first lighterman senseless into the mud.
- Quentin goes low for the second lighterman and gets nothing but stone.
- Cora throws and misses, and now has one fewer knife.
- Ursel puts their weight behind the arming sword, and the second lighterman takes all of it. _(8)_
- Quentin catches the second lighterman with the spear and something cracks. _(7)_
- Quentin follows through and opens the second lighterman up a second time. _(6)_
- Godwin's arrow skips off a wall and goes through somebody's shutter.
- Ursel gets the arming sword into the second lighterman and twists. _(10)_
- The second lighterman drops their weapon and puts both hands up. Ursel lets them go, minus their boots.
- That is the end of it. Everybody checks their hands for missing fingers.
- In the back of the bonded warehouse Cora finds 3 casks of bonded brandy, with a chalk mark on it that probably means somebody's name. _(3 casks of bonded brandy)_
- The way back up the hill feels shorter than the way down, which it is not.
- **Two weeks after they went out, the squad comes back in through the hall door. Jory counts them, and the count comes out right.**

_Home: nobody lost. Deeds: Completed a medium expedition (+2 renown)._

## Muscle for Hire: Put Lew Rudd out of business

_Long route. Outcome: complete, 14 of 14 rooms, job done._


**Week 1**

- They leave Varrow at first light by the gate the carters use, and take the road to Varrow.

**Week 2**

- They sleep in a barn one night and under a hedge the next. Varrow is still some way off.

**Week 3**

- They go out into Varrow for the long job. Jory has written all their names into the ledger in pencil.
- **They go down the hill into the Lower Town, where the streets narrow and the gutters are full.**
- They cross the fish market as it closes. Quentin is offered a herring for nothing and takes it.
- Cass finds a wire noose at ankle height and cuts it before anyone puts a foot in it. _(1 tools)_
- Cass notices a cord across the way tied to something heavy, and makes it fast before they pass. _(1 tools)_
- **They come down the river stairs onto the Quays after dark. The lighters are tied up three deep, and the warehouses have men in the doorways who do not look like warehousemen.**
- The lamps go up, and a dog starts barking two streets over and keeps at it.
- The first lighterman comes along the quay with a hook over one shoulder. "Nobody walks here after dark," they say, "except us."
- Ursel shoots, and the second lighterman moves at the last moment.
- Quentin gets the spear into the second lighterman and twists. _(6)_
- Quentin times it perfectly and the second lighterman pays for it. _(11)_
- Cass looses, and the arrow takes the second lighterman. _(8)_
- The second lighterman takes one look at how the fight is going and runs for it.
- Rosamund batters the first lighterman with the flat of the arming sword. _(5)_
- The first lighterman reels and stands there with no idea where it is.
- Quentin finds an opening and hits the first lighterman in it. _(5)_
- Quentin's second strike finds the first lighterman. _(6)_
- Cass doesn't have a clear shot and takes it anyway. It misses.
- Ursel pins the first lighterman where it stands. _(5)_
- Quentin knocks the blow aside.
- Rosamund chops at the first lighterman and connects. _(8)_
- The first lighterman drops their weapon and puts both hands up. Rosamund lets them go, minus their boots.
- The last of them goes down. Somebody laughs, too loudly.
- They eat standing up in an alley. Rosamund passes an onion round, which is generous of them, and nobody thanks them for it. _(1 food)_
- The first yard dog stands in a warehouse doorway with its head down and a growl going in its chest.
- Ursel's shot goes over the front rank and into the first yard dog. _(17, it hates that)_
- The first yard dog slams into Rosamund from the side. _(8)_
- Quentin steps in and hits the first yard dog hard with the spear. _(12, it hates that)_
- Quentin puts the first yard dog down at last.
- Quentin follows through and opens the second yard dog up a second time. _(9, it hates that)_
- Rosamund gets out of the way of the second yard dog.
- Cass picks a spot on the second yard dog and puts an arrow in it. _(15, it hates that)_
- Cass finishes the second yard dog with a blow behind the head.
- That is the end of it. Everybody checks their hands for missing fingers.

**Week 4**

- There is an empty boathouse here. Cass goes through it and finds a purse and a sack of food somebody stowed in the rafters. _(a purse of coin, 1 food)_
- Cass reads the waterline on the wall and calls a halt. The tide comes in across the way ahead and goes out again, and they walk on dry. _(1 tools)_
- **They make camp in an empty lighter under the quay wall, with the river going past a plank away.**
- They eat hot food for the first time in days. Quentin sleeps sitting up with the spoon still in hand. _(1 food)_
- **They climb into the Rookeries by a back stair behind the tanneries. The courts are six storeys deep, and every window has somebody in it.**
- Somebody has been walking behind them for a street. The first footpad stops pretending otherwise.
- Quentin catches the first footpad with the spear and something cracks. _(7)_
- Quentin comes back the other way and catches the first footpad. _(6)_
- Ursel shoots between two shoulders and hits the first footpad. _(9)_
- Ursel puts the first footpad down, and the first footpad stays down.
- Cass finds the weak place on the lighterman, and it goes in deep. _(17)_
- Rosamund puts their weight behind the arming sword, and the lighterman takes all of it. _(10)_
- The lighterman sits down hard and says they have had enough. Rosamund takes that as a yes.
- The second footpad drives the point into Rosamund's shoulder. _(9)_
- A bolt from Ursel thumps into the second footpad. _(11)_
- Cass's arrow sticks in a doorpost beside the second footpad.
- The second footpad goes for Rosamund and gets tangled up with their own side.
- Quentin swings the spear into the second footpad and keeps swinging. _(5)_
- Quentin does not wait to see what the first one did, and hits the second footpad again. _(6)_
- Quentin knocks the second footpad senseless into the mud.
- It goes quiet again, apart from something dripping.
- They come into a court with a pump in the middle and doors on every side, all shut.
- Behind a chimney breast on the roof, Cass finds 3 casks of bonded brandy. _(3 casks of bonded brandy)_

**Week 5**

- Cass spots the bowed boards, and they rope across one at a time. _(1 tools)_
- They share out bread and the end of a sausage. Ursel makes theirs last as long as it can. _(1 food)_
- They try to wait it out, and the footpads find them anyway.
- The footpad drops off a warehouse roof in front of them with a knife already out.
- **The footpad gets the first blow in before anyone sees it coming.**
- The footpad gets a blade into Quentin. _(12)_
- Rosamund gets the blow they were waiting for. The footpad feels all of it. _(14)_
- Cass picks a spot on the footpad and puts an arrow in it. _(10)_
- Cass gets the footpad on the ground, and the footpad decides that is where they will stay.
- They win that one. Nobody says anything for a while.
- They cross a court where six families are cooking on one fire. Nobody looks at them, very hard.
- **They camp on a flat roof with their backs to a chimney. The whole Rookeries is snoring below them.**
- They get a small fire going under a pot. Wounds get looked at, and people sleep where they sit. _(1 food)_
- **Lew Rudd comes out of his office into the yard wiping his hands. "You're the guild," he says. "I've hired your sort. You're dear."**
- Quentin slashes at the first yard dog and leaves it bleeding. _(11, it hates that)_
- Ursel puts an arrow into Lew Rudd, and it won't be aiming at anyone for a while. _(4)_
- Cass looses, and the arrow takes the first yard dog. _(17, it hates that)_
- Cass gets the first yard dog in the right place, and it drops.
- Rosamund brings the arming sword down on Lew Rudd. _(9)_
- Lew Rudd hits Rosamund with a fist the size of a ham. _(20)_
- Quentin lunges, slips on the wet stone and misses Lew Rudd.
- Quentin cuts again, faster, and finds Lew Rudd. _(4)_
- Ursel's arrow skips off a wall and goes through somebody's shutter.
- Rosamund chops at the second yard dog and connects. _(9)_
- The second yard dog snaps at Rosamund and gets nothing.
- Cass's shot goes over the front rank and into the second yard dog. _(15, it hates that)_
- The second yard dog gives a last kick and lies still.
- Lew Rudd swings at Quentin and hits the gatepost, which he will feel tomorrow.
- A bolt from Ursel thumps into Lew Rudd. _(9)_
- Cass shoots between two shoulders and hits Lew Rudd. _(9)_
- Quentin finds an opening and hits Lew Rudd in it. _(5)_
- Quentin follows through and opens Lew Rudd up a second time. _(5)_
- Rosamund shoves Lew Rudd back into a wall. _(3)_
- Rosamund ducks under Lew Rudd's arm. He is big, and he is slow about it.
- A bolt from Ursel thumps into Lew Rudd. _(8)_
- Rosamund slams a shoulder into Lew Rudd. _(5)_
- Quentin brings the spear down on Lew Rudd. _(5)_
- Quentin does not wait to see what the first one did, and hits Lew Rudd again. _(6)_
- Cass shoots between two shoulders and hits Lew Rudd. _(8)_
- Lew Rudd swings at Rosamund and hits the gatepost, which he will feel tomorrow.
- Quentin steps in and hits Lew Rudd hard with the spear. _(5)_
- Quentin cuts again, faster, and finds Lew Rudd. _(6)_
- Ursel looses, and the arrow takes Lew Rudd. _(10)_
- Rosamund batters Lew Rudd with the flat of the arming sword. _(3)_
- Cass's shot goes over the front rank and into Lew Rudd. _(9)_
- Lew Rudd sits down hard against the wall and says that he is out of the trade as of now.
- The fight is over. Lew Rudd's men have gone over the wall, and the dogs have gone with them.
- In a locked room off the last court Ursel finds 5 purses of old coin, stacked under the bed like somebody's savings. _(5 purses of old coin)_
- The way back up the hill feels shorter than the way down, which it is not.
- They try to slip past the footpads and get caught in the open.
- The footpad drops off a warehouse roof in front of them with a knife already out.
- **They walk straight into it. The footpad has the first move.**
- The footpad feints at Quentin and then forgets to follow it up.
- Ursel picks a spot on the footpad and puts an arrow in it. _(9)_
- Quentin catches the footpad with the spear and something cracks. _(7)_
- Quentin's second strike finds the footpad. _(6)_
- Quentin knocks the footpad senseless into the mud.
- When it is done, somebody's hands will not stop shaking, and nobody mentions it.
- **Three weeks after they went out, the squad walks back into the hall. Jory has to look twice before he writes them down.**

_Home: nobody lost. Deeds: Completed a long expedition (+4 renown); Put Lew Rudd out of business (+8 renown); Fought off 2 wandering packs (+2 renown)._
